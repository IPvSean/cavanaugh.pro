On March 3rd, 2015 I presented to a small group (maybe 30 all together) of Network Enthusiasts at Cumulus Networks East-Coast HQ (3701 NW Cary Parkway, Cary, NC) for the Triangle Network Engineers Meetup group.

If you are interested in future meetups check out one of these Meetup groups that focus on networking and automation. This was a fun talk covering Quagga routing on Cumulus Linux for network engineers who were curious about open networking.
