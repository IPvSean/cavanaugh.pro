About

Sean Cavanaugh

Senior Principal Technical Marketing Manager at Red Hat. Clemson Computer Engineering. Building enterprise AI demos that developers actually use.

Building the future of enterprise automation.

I'm a Senior Principal Technical Marketing Manager at Red Hat, focused on the intersection of AI, automation, and cloud infrastructure. I build the demos and educational content that help developers understand and adopt enterprise automation platforms.

Before Red Hat, I worked at Cumulus Networks and Cisco, where I developed a deep understanding of network infrastructure and the challenges of managing it at scale. That experience shaped my approach: make complex technology accessible through hands-on demonstrations and clear storytelling.

I've spoken at over 50 conferences including Red Hat Summit, AnsibleFest, Cisco Live, All Things Open, and AutoCon — from Atlanta to Tokyo to Seoul. My YouTube channel has grown to over 16,000 subscribers.

When I'm not building demos or on stage, I'm exploring how AI agents can transform the way we manage infrastructure — and sharing everything I learn along the way.
